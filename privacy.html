Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

  • We process your resume solely to generate analysis results.
  • Your resume text is sent to our AI provider (Groq) only to produce your analysis or rewrite.
  • The original .docx file may be kept temporarily on our server so that you can download the result. It is deleted automatically (normally within about an hour). We do not keep your resume text permanently.
  • Account data we store: your email address, a securely hashed password (if you register with a password), your credit balance, and records of your purchases (order number, amount and email).
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, we receive only your verified email address (scope: openid email). We use it solely to create and identify your account. We do not share it or use it for advertising. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Service providers: Render (hosting), Neon (database), Groq (AI processing), Lemon Squeezy (payments) and Google (sign-in) process data on our behalf and may do so outside your country, including in the United States.
  • We keep account data until you ask us to delete it.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy — we never see your card details.
  • We use a session cookie to keep you signed in. Your analysis history is stored only in your own browser (localStorage).
  • You must be 18 or older to use ResumeAI.
  • To ask us to delete your account or data, email slvm972@gmail.com.
