Quick start

  1. Upload your resume. Sign up free (email or Google) or sign in first: without an account the page shows only a sample analysis.
  2. Read the analysis: scores, strengths and improvements.
  3. Click Improve My Resume and pick a mode: Precise or Creative.
  4. Download the result as DOCX, ODT, RTF or PDF, or copy the text.

Formats

  • Input: PDF, DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT. Maximum file size is 5 MB.
  • Output: DOCX, ODT, RTF, PDF.

Languages

  • English, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew, Arabic and Chinese. The language is detected automatically, and the analysis and rewrites come back in the same language.
  • Right-to-left layout: Hebrew in DOCX, ODT, RTF and PDF; Arabic in DOCX and PDF.
  • Hebrew and Arabic are in beta: please review the improved resume carefully before using it.
  • Other languages may work, quality is not guaranteed (the analysis may come back in English).

Scores

  • Overall score (0-100) is an AI estimate of how strong the resume is. It is not a guarantee of passing any employer's system.
  • ATS score: how easily automated screening systems can read and match your resume.
  • Formatting: clarity of layout and structure.
  • Content: quality of wording and achievements.

The ring around the overall score uses three colors:

  • Green, 80-100: strong.
  • Amber, 65-79: good, with gaps.
  • Red, below 65: needs work.

Your dashboard and history use the same thresholds with more labels: Excellent 80+, Good 65-79, Fair 50-64, Poor below 50.

Modes

  • Precise: minimal safe edits. It keeps your original layout when you upload a DOCX and download a DOCX (a paragraph with complex inline formatting may be left unchanged to protect it). For other input formats the file is generated from the edited text.
  • Creative: new wording and a new summary. The layout is rebuilt, so check the change report before sending the resume. If Creative cannot produce a result that passes the safety checks, Precise edits are shown instead.

In both modes automatic checks reject added facts, numbers, employers and certificates. No automatic check is perfect, so always read the result before you send it.

Review the changes: untick any change you do not want. Your downloaded file and the copied text contain only the changes you kept. A few changes cannot be reverted automatically; they stay applied.
